What is Kpop Monkey?

Kpop Monkey is a Kpop song discovery/recommendation platform I created as a Kpop enthusiast. It's holds a collection of my personal favorite Kpop songs that I organized into a comprehensive catalogue for quick and easy listening. I'd be honored to have even one song here to make it onto your playlist!

I also share my experience with Kpop on the About Me page, along with some of my own playlists. If you notice you vibe with a lot of my song recs, or just want to hear more of a specific category, I'd recommend taking a look!

Oh, and one last thing: I tried to keep most of the music to strictly Kpop, but there are a few songs that lean more towards KR&B or Jpop. Although my most listened genre is overwhelmingly Kpop, it's not the only genre I listen to. I don't organize my playlists by genre, so it's somewhat noticeable there.
