My Kpop Experience

I first started listening to Kpop in December 2024. I liked Phonk and EDM before, but my taste in Kpop now is pretty different from those genres. I don't really care for music videos, so my music taste in Kpop is pretty much only based on how the song sounds.

I found the large majority of Kpop songs either through the Apple Music algorithm or Youtube videos. I used to spend hours searching for new music and adding it to my playlist (yup, I was kind of addicted). At some point, I calculated via Apple Music monthly recap that I spent more than 40% of my waking hours listening to Kpop.

Anyway, I don't look for music as much anymore, as I've listened to so much Kpop at this point that it's hard to find new music. But for anyone just getting into Kpop, or if you're looking to find some new music yourself, here are a few of the channels I like:

  • frromis1
  • Moonlight Seulgi
  • channierose
  • chuusays

And if you're curious, these are my favorite Kpop artists:

  1. tripleS
  2. Billlie
  3. fromis_9

Lastly, I like girl group music more than boy group music, which you may have noticed in Song Recs. I think a big part of the appeal of boy groups is supposed to be the music video, but as I said before, I don't even really watch those. Some of my favorite songs do come from boy groups, but most of the Kpop I like is from girl groups.
